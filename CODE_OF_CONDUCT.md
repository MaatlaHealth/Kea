# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We want contributing to Kea to be a good experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that make this community open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive and healthy.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and respectful to other people.
- Taking differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences seriously.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
- Owning our mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Weighing what is best for the community, not only for ourselves.
- Remembering that this project exists to improve care for pregnant women and
  adolescent girls, and that the people closest to that work often have the
  most useful things to say about it.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any
  kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political
  attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information, such as a physical or
  email address, without their explicit permission.
- **Sharing real patient data, in any form, anywhere in this project.** This
  includes issues, pull requests, screenshots, logs and test fixtures.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these
standards, and will take fair corrective action in response to behaviour they
consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and the responsibility to remove, edit or reject
comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that are not aligned
with this code of conduct, and will explain their reasons when it is
appropriate to do so.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces, and also when someone is
officially representing the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to the project
maintainers through GitHub's private reporting on the repository. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obliged to respect the privacy and security of anyone who
reports an incident.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines when deciding on consequences:

**1. Correction.** A private written warning explaining why the behaviour was
inappropriate, with a public apology requested where appropriate.

**2. Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No
interaction with the people involved for a specified period, including
unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this code. A violation during
that period may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

**3. Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction with the community
for a specified period. A violation during that period may lead to a
permanent ban.

**4. Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the
community.
